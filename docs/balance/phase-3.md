# Balance report: phase 3

Generated by `npm run balance -- --percentiles --years 2 --games 1000`, with `src/content/percentiles.json`: do not edit by hand, except under the comment at the end that says so. The numbers go stale whenever the engine or the content changes; run the command again then, and commit both files.

## What each game plays

Each difficulty plays seeds 1 to 1000, each game 104 weeks (728 days) from Monday, May 1st, year 1. The owner of seed n holds one strategy of the grid of `docs/balance/phase-2.md` from day 0, drawn at random from the seed, the same at every difficulty. The event cards come up, and the owner answers each with a choice drawn at random, seeded from the game, as `npm run sim -- --events random` does.

## How other owners did

The bénéfice net from day 0 to the close of a week's Sunday, after the owner's pay, at each percentile of the 1000 games: what « Mieux que N % des propriétaires » compares the player with, through `percentileOf()` in `src/engine/percentiles.ts`. A percentile is linear between the closest ranks (type 7, as R and NumPy compute by default), rounded half away from zero to the cent. The table shows a few weeks; `src/content/percentiles.json` holds all 104.

## Event cards

In each game, one occurrence of each card that came up is drawn at random, seeded from the game. From that morning, each choice plays 56 days, 8 weeks with the day of the card, and so does the same morning with no card, the baseline. In every branch, each business day is played with a generator seeded from the game and the day, so they all play the same customers whatever the choice drew before; each choice draws its outcome from a stream of its own. A follow-up queued comes up and is answered at random, the same way in every branch, and no new card is drawn.

- **Δ encaisse**: the Encaisse at the close of the 56th day, minus the baseline's.
- **Δ bénéfice net**: the bénéfice net over the window, minus the baseline's.
- **Median**: over the games the card came up in; for an even number, the mean of the middle two, rounded half away from zero to the cent.
- **Best on encaisse**, **Best on bénéfice net**: the share of those games where no other choice of the card did strictly better on that measure; a tie counts for each choice in it. **Best on both** and **Best on neither** follow.

A choice is too strong when it is best on both in more than 80% of the games, and too weak when it is best on neither in more than 85%. The cards are tuned at Moyen only (#101); the other difficulties are shown for information. A card that came up in few games is judged on few: the Games column says how many.

## Défis

### What each game plays

Each Défi plays seeds 1 to 1000 twice, by the owners and by the best strategy, as a player would: it opens as `startChallenge()` opens it, and the decisions change on its first morning, so they take effect from its second day. A decision the Défi locks does not change. Each morning, the card waiting is answered with a choice drawn at random among those the Défi allows, seeded from the game.

- **Owners**: the owner of seed n holds the strategy of the grid of `docs/balance/phase-2.md` that `--percentiles` draws for seed n, moved from the Défi's own opening decisions: every price moves by the strategy's % from the Défi's opening price; the croissants bought each day scale the Défi's opening quantity as the grid scales the content's starting 70, rounded half up; the marketing budget, the days open and the owner's pay are the grid's.
- **Best strategy**: of the grid's 324 strategies, moved the same way, the one whose games on seeds 1 to 100 have the best median outcome (the lower middle), then the most games that broke no condition, then the first in the grid's order.
- **Outcome**: a game that broke a condition ranks below every game that did not; the others rank by what the goal measured, where lower is better for a spoilage rate.
