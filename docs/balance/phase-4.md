## What each game plays

Each difficulty plays seeds 1 to 30 with each truck strategy below. A game opens the bakery on day 0, Monday, May 1st, year 1, with its oven leased or bought, makes its croissants there from that day on, and plays 24 months, to the close of the last day of month 24. It hires a baker that day (#158), « Boulanger » at $22.00 an hour, 16 h a week, so the owner never bakes: the baker costs a week of training on day 0 and $352.00 each Friday. Its twin plays the same seed and the same truck, buying its croissants, with no baker: each morning, the bakery makes as many as the twin buys. A strategy with contracts signs them on day 0, game and twin alike, at the terms of `src/content/grocers.json`, and renews each one as it ends; each Monday the bakery makes the deliveries' croissants first, within its capacity, those of the grocer who pays more for them first, and the twin buys them. A strategy with an employee at the truck hires one on day 0, game and twin alike, « Au camion » at $17.50 an hour, 35 h a week, $612.50 a week, who serves beside the owner. No other event card comes up. Every difficulty plays every price set below, whatever `src/content/difficulty.json` holds, and, when that file gives it prices off this grid, those too, as « shipped »; the oven and the move are `src/content/bakery.json`'s, and the wages `src/content/staff.json`'s. The truck opens 6 days a week, as the starting decisions have it, and keeps their other prices and the owner's pay.
