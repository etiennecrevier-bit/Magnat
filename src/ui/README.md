React components and browser code: reads game state and the books, never writes journal entries or holds game rules; all player-facing text is in French (Quebec).

« Camion » (`ManagePanel.tsx`) and « Équipe » (`StaffPanel.tsx`) are where the player changes decisions: they read what they type in `decisionInput.ts`, which turns down invalid text in French before it reaches the store's `decide()` and the engine's `applyDecision()`. Both keep what was typed on « ✕ » and drop it on Escape (`fieldDialog.ts`). Any panel pauses the game while it is open and resumes it at its previous speed when it closes, through the store's `openPanel(panel)` and `closePanel()`; the store's `panel` says which one is open, and a second one never opens over the first. Every panel is a modal dialog on the same cream card (`panel.css`).

## The shell: top bar, dock, panels and drawers

Nothing stays open over the street but the top bar, the dock, « Hier » and the ticker (#202). `GameScreens.tsx` is the shell App mounts under the top bar.
- **The top bar** (`TopBar.tsx`) is a few cards over the street, which takes clicks between them. At the left: a cream card with the business's name, the date, the owner's title of « Améliorations » outside a Défi, « En route vers… » on a day with a trip and the Défi's chip; the **cash chip** (`CashGauge.tsx`, `cashChip.ts`), « Encaisse 8 797 $ » to the nearest dollar (`formatWholeMoney`), a sparkline of the encaisse at the last 30 closes (`cashHistory.ts`, one balance then a walk through the days' entries) and the level as a word, « Saine », « Faible » or « Négative », whose tooltip has today's encaisse to the cent, « Frais fixes du mois » and « Ouvrir les finances », and whose click opens « Finances »; then the energy pill. At the right: the speeds, the alert chips and the gear of « Réglages ». « Valeur nette » left it for « Finances ».
- **The alert chips** (`alerts.ts`) carry an icon and words: « Livraison manquée » while a grocer's delivery was missed this week, read from the engine's record of misses (`contracts.missed`) so a miss that ended a contract counts too, which opens « Contrats »; and « 2 messages » while messages wait, which moves the focus to the newest.
- **The dock** (`Dock.tsx`, `dock.ts`, the design's `icon-*.svg` in `icons.tsx`) sits bottom centre over the street: « Camion », « Équipe », « Ville », « Finances », « Améliorations », a divider, « Journal », « Réglages », the open one in ink. C, E, V, F, A and J press them (`handleDockKey`), in either case and without a modifier, except in a text field or inside a dialog element (the panels and the event card); held down, a key does not repeat. Each button's tooltip names its key. A red count shows on « Journal » for the reports that only slid in since the player last opened one, in the full report or in « Bilans » (the store's `unreadReports`, cleared by `readReports()`); opening « Journal » with reports unread shows « Bilans » but leaves the count, and on « Finances » for each rent left unpaid in the month « Vos finances » shows, plus one while the encaisse is below zero. In a Défi, « Ville » and « Améliorations » are hidden, and so are their keys.
- **Panels** (`DockFrame.tsx`) open in the dock's frame, anchored bottom centre above it, at most 760 × 520, with their title, their tabs as pills (`.tab`) and « ✕ »: « Camion », « Équipe », « Ville » (« Aller à… » and « Contrats »), « Améliorations » and « Réglages ». They stay modal dialogs that pause the game, so the dock is out of reach while one is open. A panel the player opens closes the drawer open. Until the next cards redo their insides, « Améliorations » and « Réglages » show the panel or screen they showed before, whose own title and « Fermer » the frame hides. « Ville » keeps to the left half, above the dock, so the city stays in sight beside it (see « The city »). The Défi's panel, the event card, the report, « Magnat » and the ends stay centred.
- **Drawers** take the panels' place but only read the game, so they leave it running: « Finances » (« Entreprise », « Personnel », and in a Défi « Contrats », since « Ville » is hidden) and « Journal » (« Événements » and « Bilans »). They are not dialog elements, so Space and the dock's keys work inside them; the focus goes to the tab as one opens, and back to its dock button as it closes. Escape closes one after any tooltip took the first, never when a panel is over it, and goes no further, so the street's customer card stays. The store keeps the drawer open (`drawer`, `openDrawer()`, `closeDrawer()`, never under a panel) and each one's tab (`drawerTabs`), so the city and the alerts can open « Finances » on « Contrats » even when it shows another tab. A drawer stays on the page once opened, hidden, so its tab keeps what the player picked in it. « Finances » and « Journal » slide in from the left, 520 px wide, from under the top bar to just above the dock, so the dock, « Hier » and the ticker stay in reach (`Drawers.css`, no slide under `prefers-reduced-motion`); on a phone they are full screen, as every frame. A panel that opens on its own, such as the event card or the report, opens over the drawer, which is still there after.

- **On a phone** (under 700 px) the dock is a bar of icons the width of the window, its seven buttons sharing it, at least 44 px each down to 320 px, panels and drawers take the whole screen, the top bar wraps (the cash chip onto two rows if it must), and « Hier » folds into the cash chip's tooltip (`useMediaQuery`).

## The event cards

The store's `advance()` plays the days with the event cards on (`EVENTS`). On the morning one comes up, it stops there and opens the event card (`EventCard.tsx`), pausing the game and remembering its speed as any panel does; nothing advances until the player has chosen. A panel already open stays open and the card follows when it closes, and a saved game with an event waiting opens on the card, paused.
- **The card** shows the character's portrait, `portraits/<character id>.png` if there is one (`portraits.ts` collects them), else a cream silhouette, then their name and role, the title, the situation, their words if any, and a large button for each choice with its tags at the game's difficulty: the engine's `choiceTags()` gives the amount that applies there (as written in a Défi, see `scalingOf()`), and `eventTags.ts` writes it in place of `{montant}`, money to the dollar in fr-CA, energy in whole points and a percentage whole. The situation, the quote, the labels and the result write their amounts the same way, from the engine's `cardText()`, so « Payer 440 $ » at Moyen says what the tag and the books say. The UI never works out or shows odds. The keys 1, 2 and 3 pick the choices (`choiceKeys.ts`). The title has the focus when the card opens, so a stray Enter picks nothing. There is no close button and Escape does nothing before the choice; if the browser closes the dialog anyway, it opens again. Once the result shows, Escape does what « Continuer » does.
- **A choice** goes through the store's `chooseEvent()`, which hands it to the engine's `resolveEvent()`. The card then shows what came of it, the outcome's text and what changed, in plain lines, with « Continuer », which resumes at the speed from before the card. `eventChanges.ts` reads the lines off the game before and after the choice, without playing any rule again. What changed at once is a fact from the books: « Encaisse +78 $ », « Comptes clients +750 $ (30 juin) » with the day it is collected, « Énergie −10 », in whole points rounded as the tags round them (`wholeEnergy()`), « Vos finances +60 $ ». What comes later is one line per effect queued, never added to another, because what else happens by then is not known: « Encaisse −220 $ jeudi » for a payment or a purchase, « Comptes clients +750 $ jeudi (3 juillet) », « Encaissement de Gilles Lacasse, jeudi » with no amount, since it is what they owe on that day. A sale on credit gives no collection day when an effect still to come, queued by this choice or an earlier one, collects, writes off or delays what that client owes between the sale and that day. Stock taken later, at a cost only known on its day, follow-ups, headlines and the changes to the street get no line.
- **A notice**, a card with one choice such as Mme Prudhomme's letters before a bankruptcy, closes as soon as « Compris » is picked, by click or key, with no result to read (`pickChoice()` in `choiceKeys.ts`); the card keeps its choice until it is gone, and « Événements » in « Journal » keeps what it said.
- **The report**, the week's or the month's, goes first on a morning that also brings an event (see below).

« Événements », a tab of « Journal » (`EventLog.tsx`), lists the events of this calendar month and the 11 before, newest first, each one line with its date, its title and the choice made, which opens on click to what came of it (`JournalRow.tsx`, shared with « Bilans »), with the amounts the card showed at the game's difficulty (`eventLog.ts`). Which rows are open is the list's own, kept while the drawer is hidden but not across tabs.

The La Presse-Citron ticker (`Ticker.tsx`) runs along the bottom of the window, over the street. It shows a new headline every 20 s of real time (`MS_PER_HEADLINE` in `ticker.ts`), paused or not, and at once when a choice queues one; those come first, oldest first, then the general ones in turn. It reads `src/content/headlines.fr.json` (`headlines.ts`): `events`, the text of each headline an event queues, by its id, and `general`, at least one, with an optional `_note`. Without the file, the ticker stays hidden. A long headline wraps, and the dock, « Hier » and the panels stand above the whole bar (`tickerRoom.ts` sets `--ticker-height`).

## L'infobulle

One tooltip for the whole game (`Tooltip.tsx`), so explanations leave the screen and come on demand; the next cards move their text into it.
- **What it shows**, from `.tip` in `docs/design/reference/ui_concepts.py`: an ink card with cream text, a title in Fredoka, an optional text, an optional grid of labels and values in tabular figures, a value to look at in amber (« Trop cher 12 »), and an optional hint in amber of what clicking the anchor does, ending in « ▸ » (`TipContent` in `tooltip.ts`). The hint is not a link: the tooltip never takes the focus, so the anchor does what it says.
- **Its anchor** is drawn by a render prop that spreads the tooltip's props on it: a ref, `aria-describedby` toward the tooltip, which is always in the page, hidden while closed, and the events that open and close it. The anchor keeps its own `onClick`. The tooltip goes right after it in the page.
- **When it opens**: after 300 ms of a mouse or pen on the anchor, at once on keyboard focus, and after a 500 ms long press on touch, whose click then does nothing (`TIP` and `createTipControl()` in `tooltip.ts`). It closes when the pointer leaves, after 100 ms to cross onto the tooltip, which keeps it open, and on blur, Escape or a press elsewhere. The first Escape closes only the tooltip, not the panel or event card under it; while a hover or long press waits, Escape stops it opening.
- **Where it goes**, as the customer card does (`placeCard` in the world): right of its anchor, else left, else above, always inside the window (`placeTip()`). It takes its width first, 250 px or the window less its margins, is measured at the height its text wraps to there, and is placed again when the window, a scroll or its size changes; taller than the window, its text scrolls, by the wheel or by the arrows, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End on the focused anchor (`tipScrollTop()`), and stays out of the Tab order. Its arrow points at the anchor's middle, clear of its rounded corners. It is a manual popover, so it shows above an open panel.
- **The « (i) » button** (`InfoButton.tsx`, the design's `icon-info.svg`) carries a tooltip where nothing on the screen can; a click, Enter or a tap opens it and keeps it open until Escape, blur or a press elsewhere.

## « Hier »

« Hier » (`Yesterday.tsx`) is the small card at the bottom right: the last day's « Ventes », « Clients servis » and « Ventes perdues », a dash before the first day is over, the lost sales in amber with ⚠︎ when above zero. The cash chip's tooltip on a phone shows the same three, from the same `todayNumbers()`: customers served are the visits that reached the counter, as the engine counts them, and the lost sales are the total of its « Ventes perdues » column. A click opens « Camion ». Its corner button hides it down to an « Hier » pill that shows it again; R does both too (`foldShortcut.ts`), which both buttons' tooltips name, except in a text field or an open panel or event card; when what had the focus goes away, the button that takes its place gets it. Whether it is hidden is kept with the settings (`screensFolded`), so a reload shows it as it was, but it is not a control in « Réglages ». Where the window is too narrow for the dock and « Hier » side by side it stands above the dock, and on a phone it folds into the cash chip's tooltip.

## The « Bilan de la semaine »

The engine sums up each week at the close of its Sunday (`lastWeek`, and the last 52 in `weekHistory`) and finds the facts worth telling (`weekFacts` in `src/engine/week.ts`); the UI only words and formats them (`weeklyReport.ts`), from `src/content/report.fr.json`.
- **When it shows.** With the weekly report on, as in the app's store, `advance()` stops on the Monday morning a new `lastWeek` comes. For the first `fullReportWeeks` weeks of a game (`src/content/pacing.json`), counted from day 0 so a save loaded later counts the same, the full report opens and pauses the game as a panel does. After that, the week's card slides in at the bottom right (`WeekSlideIn.tsx`) without pausing: ventes, bénéfice, encaisse and the first fact, with « Voir le bilan », which opens the full report. It goes away `reportSlideInMs` of real time after it shows (`weekSlideIn.ts`); it hides while a panel is open and counts from the start again once the panel closes, and the next week's card replaces it. The « Ouvrir le bilan chaque semaine » switch in « Réglages » opens it in full every week; it is kept with the settings (`settings.ts`), not in the save. A game loaded on a Monday does not show that week's report again; « Bilans » has it. Tests of other parts build stores without the weekly report, whose days run on.
- **With an event.** On a Monday morning that also brings an event, the report shows first, then the event card. The game pauses for the card anyway, so the full report opens that morning even after the first weeks, in the card's place and with the same remembered speed; `closePanel()` then opens the card. A panel already open when the Monday comes stays open, and the report waits for it (`reportWaiting`), the card after it.
- **The full report** (`WeeklyReport.tsx`) shows the week's dates; « Ventes »; « Bénéfice », after the owner's pay, which the engine books as an expense; and « Encaisse » from the start of the week to its end, in fr-CA money, a loss in red with its minus sign. Then a chart of the 7 days in SVG with « Ventes » and « Argent reçu » side by side, each day telling its numbers when hovered, and a marker above the day of each sale on credit still owed, whose note follows the chart (« 750 $ de Gilles Lacasse : à recevoir le 3 août »); the facts; « Le conseil de Mme Prudhomme », a card with the banker's portrait and her line on the first fact, or a quiet one, every week (`PrudhommeAdvice.tsx`, on its own so the monthly report of R-04 can show it too); and a La Presse-Citron headline on the first fact, or a quiet one. A week always takes the same variant of each text, and the next week the next one, so the report reads the same each time it shows. In a Défi under way, a block after the figures names it, with its days played and where it stands (see « Les Défis »). After the advice, « Mieux que N % des propriétaires » compares the bénéfice net from the truck's first day to the Sunday's close with the simulated owners' at the game's difficulty and that week, rounded down (`percentileOnMorning()` in `src/engine/standing.ts`, which Facile's draw of the event cards reads too); a Défi counts its weeks from its first day.
- **« Bilans »**, a tab of « Journal » (`WeekReports.tsx`), lists the last « Bilan du mois » once one came, then the last 12 weeks of `weekHistory`, newest first, each one line with its bénéfice. A row opens on click to its report: the month's in full; the latest week's in full, with how the owner stands; an older week's in full while the store still keeps it (`weekReports`), without the percentile, title or Défi, which describe the present; otherwise its ventes, bénéfice and encaisse at the close of the Sunday. Opening one counts the reports as read.

## The « Bilan du mois »

« Réglages » sets the report « Chaque semaine » (the default) or « Chaque mois » (`reportCadence` in `settings.ts`). The first `fullReportWeeks` weeks of a game stay weekly whatever the player picked; after them, with « Chaque mois », `advance()` stops on the morning of each 1st instead of each Monday (`reportOnMorning()` in `monthlyReport.ts`), and the month's report follows the same rules as the week's: it slides in, or opens in full when « Ouvrir le bilan chaque mois » is on or the morning brings an event, and waits for a panel open. The store remembers which came last (`reportShown`), so the panel, its title and the card sliding in show that one.
- **Built in the UI, no engine change.** The engine keeps a full report only for `lastWeek`, so the store keeps each week's as it comes (`weekReports`, the last 5, as many as a month holds). They are not saved: a game started or loaded keeps only its `lastWeek`.
- **Its figures are its weeks'.** Weeks run Monday to Sunday and months do not line up with them, and a week's report has no bénéfice by day, so a month's figures are those of the weeks whose Sunday falls in it, all closed on the morning of the 1st: ventes and bénéfice are their sums in `weekHistory`, the encaisse runs from the first week's start to the last week's Sunday, and « Encaisse la plus basse » is the lowest of their reports'. The report names those weeks (« 4 semaines, du 29 mai au 25 juin, an 1 »), since they may start in the month before and end before the month does.
- **Its chart is the calendar month's**, the 1st to the last day, from the weeks kept and the days of the week under way on the 1st, the bars thinner, only Mondays labelled, with their day of the month.
- **Its facts are its weeks'.** Each kind the weeks told shows once, from the newest week it held in, the bénéfice up while the encaisse goes down first, then by weight; each line says its week (« Semaine du 5 juin — … ») and reads as that week's report wrote it, since its numbers, and what it says, are that week's. « Le conseil de Mme Prudhomme » is on the first of them, with its week; in a month with no fact, her quiet line on the last week. The Défi under way shows as in the week's report; the notes of sales on credit, the percentile and the headline are left out.
- **A game loaded during the month** has the full reports only from its `lastWeek` on: the figures still come from `weekHistory`, but « Encaisse la plus basse » is left out, the encaisse at the start too when neither the first week nor the one before it is known, the days of the weeks missing show « aucune donnée », and the facts come only from the weeks kept.

## The owner

The owner is not the company, and the UI only reads what the engine keeps of them.
- **« Énergie »** (`EnergyGauge.tsx`) sits in the top bar's energy pill beside the cash chip: a bar filled to the energy of this morning (`game.energy`) and a word, read through the engine's `fatigueOf`: « Reposé », « Fatigué » or « Épuisé », agreeing with the avatar (« Reposée » for a woman's). The bar is a meter for screen readers, and the word and an icon go with each colour.
- **« Entreprise »**, the first tab of « Finances » (`FinancesCompany.tsx`, `financesBoard.ts`), answers « how is the money? » level by level. « En un coup d'œil »: four tiles, to the dollar, each with one status line that has an arrow or a word, never colour alone, and its parts in its tooltip: « Encaisse » with ▲/▼ since the last Sunday's close (the month's opening cash and the three activities of the flux de trésorerie in the tooltip), « Ce mois-ci », the bénéfice net of the calendar month so far in parentheses when a loss, « Bénéfice » or « Perte » and the days played (ventes, charges and the biggest charge), « Valeur nette » with ▲/▼ since Sunday (`netWorthChange()`; its three parts), and « À recevoir », the comptes clients, with who pays next (up to three clients, then the total). A click on a tile opens the accordion of the statement it comes from, brings it into view and focuses it (`showFinanceSection`); « Valeur nette » moves to « Personnel », where its four parts are. Then « 30 derniers jours », the weekly report's chart (`WeekChart`) over the last 30 days played, never before the game's first day, with the days of the full reports the UI kept and of the week under way (`recentChartDays`), any other « aucune donnée », as after a save is loaded. Then « À surveiller », up to three lines with an amber icon, hidden when empty (`watchLines`): what the next 1st takes, the fixed costs the cash chip reads (`nextFixedCosts`) and the emergency loans' payments due that day; the stock lost this month (5100 Pertes sur stocks); and money owed whose payment an event's choice put off since the sale, read from `events.history` and the outcome's `delayReceivable` (`lateReceivables`), such as Gilles Lacasse's cheque « dans la malle ». Last, « Bilan », « État des résultats » and « État des flux de trésorerie », each an accordion closed by default holding one statement with its period picker (`FinancialStatements.tsx`, negatives in parentheses), and « Financement » once the game took a loan or sold shares. The store keeps which are open and each statement's period (`financeSections`), so they stay as the accordion, the tab or the drawer closes, until a new game.
- **« Personnel »**, the second tab of « Finances », shows « Vos finances », then the owner's « Valeur nette » this morning and its four parts (`netWorthParts()`): « Part de l'entreprise », « Argent personnel », « Biens personnels » and « Dettes personnelles », the debts below zero so the parts add up to it.
- **« Vos finances »**, on « Personnel » in « Finances » (`FinancesPanel.tsx`), shows the owner's personal account: the « Solde », in red below zero, then the movements of one calendar month, newest first, with an « impayé » tag on a rent that went unpaid. The account stands as of the close of the last day played, so the panel shows that day's month (`personalFinances.ts`), not the month of the day in the top bar as the financial statements do: on the morning of the 1st, it still shows the month just ended, and names it. Each « impayé » in it counts on the dock's « Finances ». Before any day is played it says « Aucun mouvement ce mois-ci. ».
- **Messages** (`Messages.tsx`) stack top centre under the top bar, newest first, at most 3, each with « Fermer »; a game imported mid-game starts with none. They are held in one stack (`messageStack.ts`), which the shell fills for each game and the top bar's « N messages » chip reads. `messageTriggers.ts` compares the game before and after each update of the store: when days were played, each rent that went unpaid brings the landlord's message, and waking up exhausted, from a morning that was not, brings the owner's own, at most once a game week (Monday to Sunday). The grocers and the employees write too (see « Les contrats, le fournil, l'équipe et le financement » below). The lines are in `src/content/messages.fr.json`, picked at random, never the last one of the same kind again. A long stretch played in one update can miss a morning that dipped below the threshold and came back; the messages are flavour, so that is acceptable.

## Les contrats, le fournil, l'équipe et le financement

The engine runs the grocers' contracts, the bakery, the employees and the financing (#156 to #159); the UI only reads them and hands the player's changes to `applyDecision()` (#163).
- **« Contrats »**, a tab of « Ville », and of « Finances » in a Défi (`ContractsScreen.tsx`, `contractsBoard.ts`), shows a card for each grocer with a contract that runs or money owed, in the content's order: the grocer's name and this week's delivery as its status line (`grocerLine`): made, with its amount (ok), missed (bad), due (warn), or the first one to come for a contract signed after this week's delivery day; « Aucun contrat en cours » for a grocer whose contract ended but who still owes. The card's tooltip (`grocerTip`) has who runs it, the croissants a week and their price, Bio-Logique's premium for those the bakery made, the weeks left, the deliveries missed against the grocers' limit, in amber once one is, and « À recevoir » with each amount and its due date, matched to the grocer by the name the journal uses. « Total à recevoir des épiceries » closes the screen, or « Aucun contrat pour l'instant. » before the first contract.
- **« Fournil »**, a section of « Camion » (`BakerySection.tsx`, `bakeryBoard.ts`), shows the bakery today, once this morning's plan is in effect (`morningBakery`), and « Demain : … » when the latest plan changes it; « Ouvrir · four loué » and « Ouvrir · four acheté », each enabled only when `applyDecision()` takes it (`accepts()`, which keeps nothing), or « Fermer le fournil » with the last day of the notice it gives, « Annuler l'ouverture » for one planned for tomorrow; the move, the rent, the lease, the oven's price or « déjà à vous », and the capacity; the unit cost of a croissant bought and made side by side; « Frais fixes du fournil », the cash each 1st takes (`bakeryMonthlyCosts`), on a line of its own, for the bakery planned or for each opening; then « Acheter / Fabriquer » and the bake rule's fields, from 0 to the capacity. Never a break-even figure.
- **« Équipe »**, a panel the dock opens (`StaffPanel.tsx`, `staffBoard.ts`), answers « who does what? »: a row for the owner and for each employee, the anchor of its tooltip, with a portrait or a silhouette (`Silhouette.tsx`), the name, the role, what they do today as chips (« Au camion », « Livraisons aux épiceries », « Fournil », « Congé », or « Ailleurs » for the owner away), and the wage: the owner's pay a week, an employee's « Salaire horaire » field. The tooltip (`ownerTip`, `memberTip`) has the role, the hours and the pay a week, the training of a hire who has not started and the notice firing one at work costs. Today's work comes from the engine's own helpers on the morning as `playDay` starts it (`startOfDay`, on a copy that is never kept, then `crewOf`, `streetOn`, `morningBakery` and the bake rule), so « Fournil » means croissants are actually made that day, and a test plays a scripted year to hold every prediction to what the engine then did. An employee hired today shows « Commence demain » as the row's status line, and cancelling costs nothing; one fired still shows today's work, and « Part demain ». « Congédier » asks « Congédier Amélie Gagnon? » with « Confirmer », which takes the focus and opens the row's tooltip with the notice at today's wage. « Embaucher » lists the roles as pills, each with its hours, starting wage and training in its tooltip (`roleTip`), then the candidates neither wanted nor at work today; at the content's most employees, the buttons are disabled with « L'équipe est complète. ».
- **« Financement »**, the last accordion of « Entreprise » in « Finances » (`FinancingBlock.tsx`, `financingSummary.ts`), shows once the game took an emergency loan or sold shares: each loan still open with what is left of it, its principal less its « Remboursement » entries, and its next payment from the engine's `chargesOn()`; « Aucun prêt d'urgence en cours » once they are paid back; each investor's share; and the next dividend, which `chargesOn()` reads on the next 1st of a quarter: « à ce jour » before it, the quarter's profit so far, and exact on that morning. Mme Prudhomme's and Max Duvent's cards are event cards (`EventCard.tsx`) with their portraits.
- **The cash chip** reads the encaisse against the bakery's rent and lease too (`monthlyFixedCosts` with the game's bakery). Wages and the emergency loans' payments stay out of it, as the engine's `monthlyFixedCosts` leaves them out.
- **Messages.** A grocer writes when a delivery is missed (`missedDelivery`), when their contract runs out (`contractEnded`) and when the last miss they allow ends it (`contractBroken`, the only message for that miss), signed « Normand, Épicerie Chez Normand »; an employee writes, by name, the morning they start (`hired`) or leave (`fired`). The employees' messages come from the « Formation » and « Préavis » entries of the days played, so someone who leaves and is hired again in one stretch of days gets both, and cancelling a hire who had not started brings none.

## Améliorations

The engine runs the two ladders and the titles (#162, `src/engine/dream.ts`); the UI reads them and hands a purchase to `applyDecision()`, which makes it that very morning.
- **« Améliorations »**, a panel the dock opens (`DreamPanel.tsx`, `dreamBoard.ts`), hidden in a Défi, as drawn in `docs/design/reference/UiAmeliorations.png` (#207): the tabs « Vie personnelle » and « Entreprise », the one shown kept in the store (`dreamTab`) while the panel is closed; an ink ribbon with the owner's title and the next one, « Débrouillard · Prochain titre : Commerçant », a bar toward it and « 27 500 $ / 100 000 $ », the last title alone; then the tab's rungs in order as cards, three a row in the full frame (1040 px wide, wider than the other panels), fewer as it narrows. Each card has its picture (`upgradeArt.ts`, `art/upgrades/<rung id>.svg`, 300 × 150, the owner's copied from the reference, the company's drawn in the same style), its name and its price to the dollar, its bonus in a few words as a green chip (`bonusChip`), « −30 % de trajet », « Loyer remplacé », « +15 d'énergie par congé », then a foot that says where it stands: « Acheter » on the next rung when the engine buys it (`buys()`, which keeps nothing), else a bar with « 8 000 $ requis · 2 500 $ disponibles », the condo sold back counting for the house; « Acquis » or « Revendu » with a dot; a padlock on the faded picture and « Après : Voiture d'occasion » for a rung after the next one, « Bientôt » for one not for sale yet. Each card is the anchor of its tooltip (`rungTip`): the price and who pays, then what it gives in plain facts from the content's numbers, « Déplacements en ville : −30 % de temps », « Hypothèque du condo : 1 100,00 $ par mois », « L'ancien camion repris à sa valeur comptable : … », and on the next rung, « Il vous manque » in amber. « Acheter » asks to confirm on the card with the price, who pays and the same facts. It never explains why a rung is worth it.
- **The title** shows under the date in the top bar, outside a Défi, and in the « Bilan de la semaine » as « Titre : … », from the net worth of its Sunday's close.
- **« Magnat »** (`MagnatScreen.tsx`) opens on its own the morning after the close at which the net worth first reached the last title, with the business, the net worth and « Continuer »; the store opens that morning's report, then its event card, after it. The game goes on.
- « Camion » takes the restock rules up to what the bigger truck allows (`dreamLimits()`), and « Vos finances » tags a home's payment that went unpaid « impayé », as a rent.

## Aller à…

The engine sends the owner into town (#164, `src/engine/places.ts`); the UI reads it and hands a trip to `applyDecision()`, which makes it that very morning.
- **« Aller à… »**, the first tab of « Ville », a panel the dock opens (`TripPanel.tsx`, `tripBoard.ts`), hidden in a Défi: a row for each place, the anchor of its tooltip, with « Trajet : 25 min · visite : 30 min » with the vehicle's minutes, or « Toute la journée », and one status line (`placeLine`): « Fermé pendant ce temps » when the truck opens today and no employee works at it. The tooltip (`placeTip`) has what the owner does there, what it gives and « Départ 10 h · retour 11 h 20 ». « Y aller » is enabled only when the engine takes the trip (`tripRefusal()`), the reason shown otherwise as the status line, such as « Déjà reçu cette année » or, for the permits office, « Rien à déposer »; the day's trip shows the chip « Aujourd'hui » and « Annuler », disabled with « Déplacement obligatoire » on the day a card booked, such as « Changer de coin de rue ». The event card shows no change line for a booked trip, as for a closed day.
- **The top bar** shows « En route vers la Caisse », the place's `towards`, under the date on a day with a trip, chosen or booked (`enRouteLine()`).

## The city

- **« Ville »** shows the city: while it is open, the world draws the city behind it, and the street again once it closes, however it does (`showCity`, which sets `worldView` in the settings store; it is never kept, so a game always starts on the street). The top bar's « La rue » and « La ville » of #165 are gone. The panel stays a modal dialog, so a press on the city lands on its backdrop, which hands it to the city (`DockPanel`'s `backdrop`, `backdropPoint`), the pointer showing over a building that opens something; such a press moves no focus. To leave the buildings in sight, the panel keeps to the left half of the window, since they stand in the city's right half; on a phone it comes down from the top and stops above the city, which is as wide as the window and stands on the band, and the dock hides (`TripPanel.css`).
- **A click on a building** comes from the world as a link, which `cityLinks.ts` opens as the dock would. With « Ville » open: a place moves it to « Aller à… », a grocer's shop to « Contrats » on that grocer's card (the store's `pickGrocer`, which counts each pick, so the same shop clicked again brings the card again), and the bakery swaps it for « Camion » at « Fournil ». The card comes into view, takes the focus and opens its tooltip (`TipActions.open`, since a focus that follows a press does not open one), once per pick: then `settleCityPick` marks the pick done, so a return to « Contrats » later leaves the focus on its tab, the card still there; a grocer with no contract and nothing owed gets a card of their own there (`withGrocer`), « Aucun contrat en cours ». With no panel open: « Ville » for a place, outside a Défi; « Camion » at « Fournil » for the bakery (the store's `openPanel('manage', 'bakery')`); and « Contrats » for a grocer (`openContracts()`): « Ville » on its « Contrats » tab, or, in a Défi, « Finances » on « Contrats » (`openDrawer('finances', 'contracts')`), which leaves the game running. Another panel already open stays, as with the dock's buttons, and nothing opens before a game starts.

## The street's spots

- **A click on a spot of the street** (#208) comes from the world as a link, `{ dock, tab? }`, which `streetLinks.ts` opens as that dock button would, on « Camion »'s tab when it names one: nothing while a panel is open, nor « Ville » in a Défi. `openCityLink` passes street links on to it, so the world reports both kinds through one `onOpen`.
- **The legend** « Cliquez sur la rue » shows in the player's first game only, for its first three days. `streetLegend.ts`, started by `GameScreens.tsx`, keeps in the settings (`streetLegend`, not the save) which game that is, by its seed: `unseen`, then that game's seed, then `done` once it is past its days, a new game starts (`isNewGame` in `store.ts`: a seed alone cannot tell two games of the same `?seed=` apart) or a loaded game of another seed plays, or straight away for a save already past them. Continuing the first game from its save keeps it.

## Starting and saving a game

`src/App.tsx` opens on the start screen (`StartScreen.tsx`) over the world. The top bar, the shell, the game loop and the autosave only start once a game does, so the menu never advances time or overwrites the saved game.
- **Continuer** shows only when the game saved in this browser loads. A save that no longer loads, or a browser that blocks storage, gets a notice instead.
- **Défis**, right after « Nouvelle partie », opens the Défis (see « Les Défis » below).
- **Nouvelle partie** asks for a difficulty (`src/content/difficulty.json`), a business name and an avatar (defaults in `src/content/profile.json`), then starts on a random seed from `crypto.getRandomValues`, or on the page's `?seed=`.
- **The player's first new game** offers Réal's tour first (« Réal t'attend au camion. », `TourOfferView`): « Faire la visite » starts the tutorial game (`newTutorialGame()` in `store.ts`, on the tutorial's own seed, at the difficulty picked), « Passer » the new game. Either way the settings keep `tutorialOffered`, so later games start without the offer (`startFromForm()` and `answerTourOffer()` in `startMenu.ts`); settings kept before the tour existed count a player whose first game took the street's legend as offered. A Défi never offers it.

**« Réglages »** (`SettingsPanel.tsx`), from the gear at the right end of the top bar or the dock's last button, is a panel like the others, so the game pauses while it is open. Each setting lives there and nowhere else:
- **« Effets visuels »**: « Complets » (the default) or « Simples », for slower computers. The street behind the panel shows the change at once (see `src/world/README.md`).
- **« Bilan »**: « Chaque semaine » or « Chaque mois », and the switch « Ouvrir le bilan chaque semaine », or « chaque mois » (see « Le Bilan de la semaine » and « Le Bilan du mois » above).
- **« Exporter la partie »** downloads the game played as a file.
- **« Refaire la visite »**, hidden in a Défi, asks to confirm, then saves the game played at once, through the autosave's writer that `App.tsx` hands down, and starts Réal's tour in its place with `keepsSave` (`replayTour()` in `settingsPanel.ts`). The autosave leaves the browser's save alone while that tour is protected, so « Continuer » keeps the game saved before it, and saves the tour, replacing it, the moment its protection ends: on day 8, or as it is skipped. When the save fails, nothing starts and the panel says so; « Refaire quand même » then starts the tour as any new game, replacing the save.
- **« Importer une partie »** loads an exported file through the engine's `deserialize()`, and shows a French message when the file is rejected. Only the last file picked counts, and only while the panel is open (`settingsPanel.ts`). The game it holds replaces the one played, through the store's `startGame()`, which closes the panel; an ordinary game also replaces the browser's save at once (see below).

The settings are kept in `localStorage` (`settings.ts`, key `magnat.reglages`), not in the save. The same key keeps the best medal won in each Défi and whether « Hier » is hidden; each setting reads on its own, so one this version does not know, or an older version did not keep, falls back to its default and leaves the others as they are.

`autosave.ts` saves the game to `localStorage` (`saves.ts`, key `magnat.sauvegarde`) when it starts, on the first day of each game month and whenever the tab is hidden. A game started in place of the one played, such as one imported, is saved at once: the store counts the games it starts (`gamesStarted`), and the autosave tells the tab's save writer that game `replaces` the saved one, so it replaces the browser's save whatever another tab saved, as starting one from the menu does. A replacement that fails is left to the next save. Every other save keeps to the rule between tabs: of two tabs on one game, the one that played it furthest keeps the save, and a tab never writes over a game another tab started. It never saves a Défi: the browser keeps one game, and trying a Défi must not replace the long one. A game that went bankrupt is saved once, the morning it ends, or when it is started in place of another, and never again, so « Continuer » shows its « Faillite », and the start menu names it « Chez Mimi, faillite le 30 novembre, an 1 » (`savedLine()` in `startMenu.ts`); one continued already ended is not saved again, and « Recommencer » saves the new game in its place. Every storage access is caught: when a save fails, the game keeps going and tells the player to export the game from « Réglages ».

## La visite de Réal

The tutorial (#236 for the engine, #238 for the UI): Réal Gingras walks the player through seven protected days, one gesture at a time. What he says, where each step points and every other text of the tour come from `src/content/tutorial.json`.
- **The steps** are the engine's: `tutorialStepOf()` names the step due on the morning, and `tick()` refuses a day while one is due. The store never plays a day while a step is due, and stops on the morning one comes, paused, with the drawer closed so nothing covers its target; a panel that opens that morning, such as the day-4 event card, resumes paused. `completeTutorialStep()` marks the step done through `applyDecision({ kind: 'tutorialStep' })`, only when it is the one due; once no step is left that day, the game goes on at ×1, at once or as the panel open closes. `skipTour()` ends the tour through `skipTutorial()`, at ×1. A save loaded mid-tour starts paused on its step.
- **The gestures** (`tutorialFlow.ts`): `watchTutorial()`, started by `GameScreens.tsx`, reads the store's changes for the step due before each change (« Camion » or « Finances » opened, a price or the croissant rule changed, a speed picked, a choice made, « Hier » opening « Camion » on « Menu »), already done when the step comes for « Camion » and « Finances », and the signals of `tutorialSignals.ts`: a tooltip opening on an anchor of the step's `tipAnchors`, and a speed button pressed, even on the speed already picked, so a save loaded on the pause step still completes. The bubble's button completes `next` and `priceOrOk`, and a step's `fallback` when `tutorialFacts()` says its fact did not happen the day before. After the pause, the game goes on at ×1 by itself `resumeAfterPauseMs` later, unless the player picked a speed, a panel opened or another game started.
- **The bubble** (`CoachBubble.tsx`, placed by `coachBubble.ts`): Réal's portrait, 64 px and round (the silhouette until #237), his name, his text, at most one button and « Passer la visite », which asks once to confirm. Each frame it finds the first anchor of the step, by `data-tuto`, the player can reach: on the page, shown, in the topmost modal dialog when one is open, not covered, and brought into view once when what scrolls around it clips it. It goes beside it, right, left, above, then below, inside the window, with its arrow toward it; on a phone, below it or above, full width less 16 px. The window dims, ink at 35 %, but a rounded hole around the target, and four blocks keep every press outside the hole from the game; a step whose gesture is the bubble's button only shows its target, so the hole is blocked too. With no anchor, the bubble is centred over the whole window dimmed. With anchors but none in reach, it neither dims nor blocks, and waits at the top, so the player can always close what covers the target. A press on a target whose tooltip completes the step opens that tooltip, as a tap on a phone does not. The layer is a manual popover on the top layer, inside the open modal dialog when there is one, outside which the page is inert. On a screen that cannot hover, a step's `touchText` shows.
- **Keyboard and screen readers**: the bubble is a dialog that is not modal, named by Réal's name and described by his text, which a live region reads out as each step comes. The live region moves into the topmost modal dialog while one is open, since the page outside it is inert, and takes its text a moment after it moves, so the step that comes as « Camion » opens is still read (`CoachAnnouncer`, `ANNOUNCE_DELAY_MS`). Tab cycles between the target and the bubble's controls; when the target changes or focus moves, focus left outside both, such as on the control of the step before or on a panel's first tab, goes to the target, or to the bubble where the target only shows or opens a tooltip (`refocus()`); Escape does nothing but back out of « Passer la visite »; the dock's keys, R and Space wait while the bubble shows (`coachShowing()`).
- **Anchors**: `dock.<item>`, `speed.<n>`, `cash.chip`, `yesterday` and `yesterday.pill`, `menu.<product>` on a product card and `menu.<product>.price` on its field, `stocks.<product>`, `tab.<tab>` and `panel.close` or `drawer.close` in the dock's frames, `event.choices` and `event.card`.
- **Never in a Défi**: a Défi has no tutorial, and the bubble, the offer and « Refaire la visite » never show there.

## La faillite

Six whole months in a row with the encaisse below zero end the game (#160, `src/engine/bankruptcy.ts`). The engine plays no day after, so `advance()` stops on the morning after the close that ends it and opens the « Faillite » (`BankruptcyEnd.tsx`) in place of that morning's report, pausing the game for good: nothing closes it but its buttons, as the end of a Défi. A game loaded already ended opens on it too. It shows the business and « Dossier fermé le 30 novembre, an 1 », « Mieux que N % des propriétaires » (`percentileOnMorning()`), rounded down, the key numbers (`bankruptcyFigures()` in `bankruptcyEnd.ts`): « Jours joués », « Meilleure semaine », the best weekly ventes reported, « Ventes » and « Bénéfice net » from the état des résultats from day 0, and « Valeur nette » (`netWorth()` in `src/engine/netWorth.ts`); then « Le mot de Mme Prudhomme », with her portrait, the line of `src/content/bankruptcy.fr.json` the game's seed picks, so a reload shows the same; then « Recommencer », a new game for the same business, name, avatar and difficulty, on a new seed, and « Retour au menu ». It explains nothing.

## Les Défis

The engine plays and judges the Défis (`src/engine/challenge.ts`, content in `src/content/defis.json`); the UI only words and formats what it keeps (`defis.ts`), reusing the engine's `isLocked`, `allowedChoices`, `failedCondition` and `metricValue` rather than any rule of its own.
- **The list** (`DefisScreen.tsx`), from « Défis » on the start menu, shows each Défi in the content's order with its title, difficulty, length (« 3 semaines », or « 64 jours » when not whole weeks) and the best medal won, or « Aucune médaille ». The medals are kept with the settings (`settings.ts`, `bestMedals`), not in a save, and only a better medal replaces one: each write of the settings keeps the better of the stored and the tab's medal in each Défi, so a tab left open never erases or lowers a medal another tab won.
- **The briefing** tells the story, how the game opens (the difficulty's line with the Défi's cash), its dates, the goal with the threshold of each medal, its conditions, what it locks (a line per kind of decision, and per event card it forces, with the choices left), the best medal won, and that a Défi is not saved. « Commencer » starts it through the store's `newChallenge`, on the page's `?seed=` or a random seed, with the default business name and avatar.
- **In game**, a chip under the date in the top bar reads « Défi · semaine 2 de 3 », its weeks counted from the Défi's first day, and opens the Défi's panel (`DefiPanel.tsx`): the goal, where it stands (« Encaisse la plus basse : 1 870 $ », one line per measure the goal and the conditions watch), the thresholds and the conditions. Amounts never round to a threshold they miss: the energy rounds down, the share of goods thrown out rounds up to a tenth of a percent. The cash chip counts the loan's months from the Défi's first day, as the engine does.
- **Locked decisions** are disabled in « Camion », with the reason under the section's title (« Le Défi fixe l'horaire. »), which the controls point to. On an event card the Défi forces, the choices it leaves out are disabled, with « Le Défi impose un autre choix. », and their number key does nothing.
- **The end.** The engine plays no day once the Défi is over, so `advance()` stops on the morning after the day that ends it, its last or the one a condition broke, and opens the end (`DefiEnd.tsx`) in place of that morning's report, pausing the game for good: nothing closes it but its buttons. It says « Défi réussi » with the medal, or « Défi échoué » with the condition broken or how far the goal fell short of bronze, then the key numbers and the days played, « Rejouer », the same Défi on a new seed for the same business, and « Retour au menu », back to the start screen (App's `onMenu`), whose « Continuer » offers the long game as it was saved. Nothing is lost, as the Défi is not saved and its medal is already kept. A Défi over that is loaded opens on its end too. Under the verdict, « Mieux que N % des parties simulées » compares the Défi with the simulated games of `src/content/defis-percentiles.json`, rounded down (`defiPercentileOf()` in `src/engine/percentiles.ts`): a Défi that broke a condition is better than none of them, and one that broke none ranks above every game that broke one, then by its goal's value among the others. `GameScreens` hands every medal won to the settings (`keepBestMedals`), including that of a Défi won and then loaded.
- **Saving.** A Défi is never autosaved, so the long game stays in the browser; « Exporter la partie » still exports it, and an imported Défi plays as one.
