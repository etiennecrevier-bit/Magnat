React components and browser code: reads game state and the books, never writes journal entries or holds game rules; all player-facing text is in French (Quebec).

The « Gérer le camion » panel (`ManagePanel.tsx`) is the only place the player changes decisions: it reads what they type in `decisionInput.ts`, which turns down invalid text in French before it reaches the store's `decide()` and the engine's `applyDecision()`. Any panel pauses the game while it is open and resumes it at its previous speed when it closes, through the store's `openPanel(panel)` and `closePanel()`; the store's `panel` says which one is open, and a second one never opens over the first. Every panel is a modal dialog on the same cream card (`panel.css`).

## The event cards

The store's `advance()` plays the days with the event cards on (`EVENTS`). On the morning one comes up, it stops there and opens the event card (`EventCard.tsx`), pausing the game and remembering its speed as any panel does; nothing advances until the player has chosen. A panel already open stays open and the card follows when it closes, and a saved game with an event waiting opens on the card, paused.
- **The card** shows the character's portrait, `portraits/<character id>.png` if there is one (`portraits.ts` collects them), else a cream silhouette, then their name and role, the title, the situation, their words if any, and a large button for each choice with its tags at the game's difficulty: the engine's `choiceTags()` gives the amount that applies there (as written in a Défi, see `scalingOf()`), and `eventTags.ts` writes it in place of `{montant}`, money to the dollar in fr-CA, energy in whole points and a percentage whole. The situation, the quote, the labels and the result write their amounts the same way, from the engine's `cardText()`, so « Payer 440 $ » at Moyen says what the tag and the books say. The UI never works out or shows odds. The keys 1, 2 and 3 pick the choices (`choiceKeys.ts`). The title has the focus when the card opens, so a stray Enter picks nothing. There is no close button and Escape does nothing before the choice; if the browser closes the dialog anyway, it opens again. Once the result shows, Escape does what « Continuer » does.
- **A choice** goes through the store's `chooseEvent()`, which hands it to the engine's `resolveEvent()`. The card then shows what came of it, the outcome's text and what changed, in plain lines, with « Continuer », which resumes at the speed from before the card. `eventChanges.ts` reads the lines off the game before and after the choice, without playing any rule again. What changed at once is a fact from the books: « Encaisse +78 $ », « Comptes clients +750 $ (30 juin) » with the day it is collected, « Énergie −10 », in whole points rounded as the tags round them (`wholeEnergy()`), « Vos finances +60 $ ». What comes later is one line per effect queued, never added to another, because what else happens by then is not known: « Encaisse −220 $ jeudi » for a payment or a purchase, « Comptes clients +750 $ jeudi (3 juillet) », « Encaissement de Gilles Lacasse, jeudi » with no amount, since it is what they owe on that day. A sale on credit gives no collection day when an effect still to come, queued by this choice or an earlier one, collects, writes off or delays what that client owes between the sale and that day. Stock taken later, at a cost only known on its day, follow-ups, headlines and the changes to the street get no line.
- **The weekly report** goes first on a Monday morning that also brings an event (see below).

« Journal des événements », the third screen (`EventLog.tsx`), lists the events of this calendar month and the 11 before, newest first, each with its date, the choice made and what came of it, with the amounts the card showed at the game's difficulty (`eventLog.ts`).

The La Presse-Citron ticker (`Ticker.tsx`) runs along the bottom of the window, over the street. It shows a new headline every 20 s of real time (`MS_PER_HEADLINE` in `ticker.ts`), paused or not, and at once when a choice queues one; those come first, oldest first, then the general ones in turn. It reads `src/content/headlines.fr.json` (`headlines.ts`): `events`, the text of each headline an event queues, by its id, and `general`, at least one, with an optional `_note`. Without the file, the ticker stays hidden. A long headline wraps, and the page keeps room under the screens for the whole bar (`tickerRoom.ts`).

## The « Bilan de la semaine »

The engine sums up each week at the close of its Sunday (`lastWeek`, and the last 52 in `weekHistory`) and finds the facts worth telling (`weekFacts` in `src/engine/week.ts`); the UI only words and formats them (`weeklyReport.ts`), from `src/content/report.fr.json`.
- **When it shows.** With the weekly report on, as in the app's store, `advance()` stops on the Monday morning a new `lastWeek` comes. For the first `fullReportWeeks` weeks of a game (`src/content/pacing.json`), counted from day 0 so a save loaded later counts the same, the full report opens and pauses the game as a panel does. After that, the week's card slides in at the bottom right (`WeekSlideIn.tsx`) without pausing: ventes, bénéfice, encaisse and the first fact, with « Voir le bilan », which opens the full report. It goes away `reportSlideInMs` of real time after it shows (`weekSlideIn.ts`); it hides while a panel is open and counts from the start again once the panel closes, and the next week's card replaces it. The « Ouvrir le bilan chaque semaine » switch on the report opens it in full every week; it is kept with the settings (`settings.ts`), not in the save. A game loaded on a Monday does not show that week's report again; « Bilans » has it. Tests of other parts build stores without the weekly report, whose days run on.
- **With an event.** On a Monday morning that also brings an event, the report shows first, then the event card. The game pauses for the card anyway, so the full report opens that morning even after the first weeks, in the card's place and with the same remembered speed; `closePanel()` then opens the card. A panel already open when the Monday comes stays open, and the report waits for it (`reportWaiting`), the card after it.
- **The full report** (`WeeklyReport.tsx`) shows the week's dates; « Ventes »; « Bénéfice », after the owner's pay, which the engine books as an expense; and « Encaisse » from the start of the week to its end, in fr-CA money, a loss in red with its minus sign. Then a chart of the 7 days in SVG with « Ventes » and « Argent reçu » side by side, each day telling its numbers when hovered, and a marker above the day of each sale on credit still owed, whose note follows the chart (« 750 $ de Gilles Lacasse : à recevoir le 3 août »); the facts; and a La Presse-Citron headline on the first fact, or a quiet one. A week always takes the same variant of each text, and the next week the next one, so the report reads the same each time it shows. In a Défi under way, a block after the figures names it, with its days played and where it stands (see « Les Défis »). The percentile (P3-13, #101) is not shown yet.
- **« Bilans »**, the fourth screen (`WeekReports.tsx`), lists the last 12 weeks of `weekHistory`, newest first, with their ventes, bénéfice and encaisse at the close of the Sunday, then the latest week's full report.

## The owner

The owner is not the company, and the UI only reads what the engine keeps of them.
- **« Énergie »** (`EnergyGauge.tsx`) sits in the top bar beside the encaisse: a bar filled to the energy of this morning (`game.energy`) and a word, read through the engine's `fatigueOf`: « Reposé », « Fatigué » or « Épuisé », agreeing with the avatar (« Reposée » for a woman's). The bar is a meter for screen readers, and the word and an icon go with each colour.
- **« Vos finances »** (`FinancesPanel.tsx`) shows the owner's personal account: the « Solde », in red below zero, then the movements of one calendar month, newest first, with an « impayé » tag on a rent that went unpaid. The account stands as of the close of the last day played, so the panel shows that day's month (`personalFinances.ts`), not the month of the day in the top bar as the financial statements do: on the morning of the 1st, it still shows the month just ended, and names it. Before any day is played it says « Aucun mouvement ce mois-ci. ».
- **Messages** (`Messages.tsx`) show at the top of the screens, newest first, at most 3, each with « Fermer ». `messageTriggers.ts` compares the game before and after each update of the store: when days were played, each rent that went unpaid brings the landlord's message, and waking up exhausted, from a morning that was not, brings the owner's own, at most once a game week (Monday to Sunday). The lines are in `src/content/messages.fr.json`, picked at random, never the last one of the same kind again. A long stretch played in one update can miss a morning that dipped below the threshold and came back; the messages are flavour, so that is acceptable.

## Starting and saving a game

`src/App.tsx` opens on the start screen (`StartScreen.tsx`) over the world. The top bar, the screens, the game loop and the autosave only start once a game does, so the menu never advances time or overwrites the saved game.
- **Continuer** shows only when the game saved in this browser loads. A save that no longer loads, or a browser that blocks storage, gets a notice instead.
- **Défis**, right after « Nouvelle partie », opens the Défis (see « Les Défis » below).
- **Nouvelle partie** asks for a difficulty (`src/content/difficulty.json`), a business name and an avatar (defaults in `src/content/profile.json`), then starts on a random seed from `crypto.getRandomValues`, or on the page's `?seed=`.
- **Importer une sauvegarde** loads an exported file through the engine's `deserialize()`, and shows a French message when the file is rejected. Only the last file picked counts, and only while the player is still on the menu: picking another file, continuing, opening the new game form or starting a game drops the file being read (`startMenu.ts`).
- **« Effets visuels »**, under the buttons: « Complets » (the default) or « Simples », for slower computers. The choice is kept in `localStorage` (`settings.ts`, key `magnat.reglages`), and the world behind the menu shows it at once (see `src/world/README.md`). During a game the menu is gone, so the setting changes back there, after a reload. The same key keeps « Ouvrir le bilan chaque semaine » and the best medal won in each Défi; each setting reads on its own, so one this version does not know, or an older version did not keep, falls back to its default and leaves the others as they are.

`autosave.ts` saves the game to `localStorage` (`saves.ts`, key `magnat.sauvegarde`) when it starts, on the first day of each game month and whenever the tab is hidden. It never saves a Défi: the browser keeps one game, and trying a Défi must not replace the long one. Every storage access is caught: when a save fails, the game keeps going and tells the player to use « Exporter la sauvegarde », which downloads the game as a file.

## Les Défis

The engine plays and judges the Défis (`src/engine/challenge.ts`, content in `src/content/defis.json`); the UI only words and formats what it keeps (`defis.ts`), reusing the engine's `isLocked`, `allowedChoices`, `failedCondition` and `metricValue` rather than any rule of its own.
- **The list** (`DefisScreen.tsx`), from « Défis » on the start menu, shows each Défi in the content's order with its title, difficulty, length (« 3 semaines », or « 64 jours » when not whole weeks) and the best medal won, or « Aucune médaille ». The medals are kept with the settings (`settings.ts`, `bestMedals`), not in a save, and only a better medal replaces one: each write of the settings keeps the better of the stored and the tab's medal in each Défi, so a tab left open never erases or lowers a medal another tab won.
- **The briefing** tells the story, how the game opens (the difficulty's line with the Défi's cash), its dates, the goal with the threshold of each medal, its conditions, what it locks (a line per kind of decision, and per event card it forces, with the choices left), the best medal won, and that a Défi is not saved. « Commencer » starts it through the store's `newChallenge`, on the page's `?seed=` or a random seed, with the default business name and avatar.
- **In game**, a chip under the date in the top bar reads « Défi · semaine 2 de 3 », its weeks counted from the Défi's first day, and opens the Défi's panel (`DefiPanel.tsx`): the goal, where it stands (« Encaisse la plus basse : 1 870 $ », one line per measure the goal and the conditions watch), the thresholds and the conditions. Amounts never round to a threshold they miss: the energy rounds down, the share of goods thrown out rounds up to a tenth of a percent. The cash gauge counts the loan's months from the Défi's first day, as the engine does.
- **Locked decisions** are disabled in « Gérer le camion », with the reason under the section's title (« Le Défi fixe l'horaire. »), which the controls point to. On an event card the Défi forces, the choices it leaves out are disabled, with « Le Défi impose un autre choix. », and their number key does nothing.
- **The end.** The engine plays no day once the Défi is over, so `advance()` stops on the morning after the day that ends it, its last or the one a condition broke, and opens the end (`DefiEnd.tsx`) in place of that morning's report, pausing the game for good: nothing closes it but its buttons. It says « Défi réussi » with the medal, or « Défi échoué » with the condition broken or how far the goal fell short of bronze, then the key numbers and the days played, « Rejouer », the same Défi on a new seed for the same business, and « Retour au menu », back to the start screen (App's `onMenu`), whose « Continuer » offers the long game as it was saved. Nothing is lost, as the Défi is not saved and its medal is already kept. A Défi over that is loaded opens on its end too. « Mieux que N % des parties simulées » waits for P3-14 (#102). `GameScreens` hands every medal won to the settings (`keepBestMedals`), including that of a Défi won and then loaded.
- **Saving.** A Défi is never autosaved, so the long game stays in the browser; « Exporter la sauvegarde » still exports it, and an imported Défi plays as one.
