Pure, deterministic TypeScript game logic: randomness comes only from the seeded RNG in `rng.ts` and game time advances only through `tick()`; it never imports React, the DOM or anything from `src/ui/`.

`types.ts` is the contract the engine, the books, the UI and the tools share. It is frozen at the end of phase 1: changing it needs a dedicated card. `docs/architecture.md` describes the state, the order of a day and how the books are written.

`scaling.ts` plays the same event card differently at each difficulty (#137): `effectsAt()` keeps the effects that apply at the game's difficulty and scales their gains and losses as `events.json`'s `scaling` sets, money to the cent and energy to the whole point, and `choiceTags()` and `cardText()` give the amounts the tags and the card's text show, which the UI formats. A Défi plays the cards as written (`scalingOf()` in `events.ts`), since its medals were tuned on them. `standing.ts` reads the player's standing each morning, « en difficulté », « dans la moyenne » or « en avance », from the encaisse against `fixedCosts.ts`'s month of fixed costs and from where the player stands among the simulated owners, `percentileOnMorning()`, which the « Bilan de la semaine » also shows; `drawEvent()` weighs the cards by it at the difficulties of `adaptiveDraw`, but in a Défi, against the shipped percentile table unless `tick()` is given another, as `npm run balance -- --percentiles` does to build Facile's table week by week. It is read from the state, so nothing goes in the save.

`bakery.ts` runs the bakery, le fournil (#157): `bakeryMorning()` opens it, charges its rent, its oven's lease and depreciation, gives and takes back its notice, and `bake()` makes the croissants at what their ingredients cost; `day.ts` calls both. `morningBakery()` is the bakery a morning leaves, with nothing posted, which `applyDecision()` and `deserialize()` check the next morning's plan against. `posting.ts` holds the helpers both files post their entries with.

`percentiles.ts` tells where an owner stands among the simulated owners of `src/content/percentiles.json`, which `npm run balance -- --percentiles` generates (see `tools/README.md`); the « Bilan de la semaine » shows it through `percentileOnMorning()`. `defiPercentileOf()` tells where a Défi over stands among the simulated games of `src/content/defis-percentiles.json`, which `npm run balance -- --defis` generates; the end of a Défi shows it.
