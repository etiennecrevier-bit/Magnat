Pure, deterministic TypeScript game logic: randomness comes only from the seeded RNG in `rng.ts` and game time advances only through `tick()`; it never imports React, the DOM or anything from `src/ui/`.

`types.ts` is the contract the engine, the books, the UI and the tools share. It is frozen at the end of phase 1: changing it needs a dedicated card. `docs/architecture.md` describes the state, the order of a day and how the books are written.

`scaling.ts` plays the same event card differently at each difficulty (#137): `effectsAt()` keeps the effects that apply at the game's difficulty and scales their gains and losses as `events.json`'s `scaling` sets, money to the cent and energy to the whole point, and `choiceTags()` and `cardText()` give the amounts the tags and the card's text show, which the UI formats. A Défi plays the cards as written (`scalingOf()` in `events.ts`), since its medals were tuned on them. `standing.ts` reads the player's standing each morning, « en difficulté », « dans la moyenne » or « en avance », from the encaisse against `fixedCosts.ts`'s month of fixed costs and from where the player stands among the simulated owners, `percentileOnMorning()`, which the « Bilan de la semaine » also shows; `drawEvent()` weighs the cards by it at the difficulties of `adaptiveDraw`, but in a Défi, against the shipped percentile table unless `tick()` is given another, as `npm run balance -- --percentiles` does to build Facile's table week by week. It is read from the state, so nothing goes in the save.

`bakery.ts` runs the bakery, le fournil (#157): `bakeryMorning()` opens it, charges its rent, its oven's lease and depreciation, gives and takes back its notice, and `bake()` makes the croissants at what their ingredients cost; `day.ts` calls both. `morningBakery()` is the bakery a morning leaves, with nothing posted, which `applyDecision()` and `deserialize()` check the next morning's plan against. `posting.ts` holds the helpers both files post their entries with.

`contracts.ts` runs the grocers' weekly croissant contracts (#156): `startContract()` signs one and `grocerAway()` keeps a grocer away, both from an event card's choice, `mayOffer()` is the cards' `grocer` condition, and `deliverContracts()` delivers each Monday, open or closed, a batch of its own the bakery makes first and the supplier completes, sold on credit to the grocer; `day.ts` calls it after the bakery's morning. It loads `grocers.json` as `GROCERS` against the event cards it loads itself, so it never imports `day.ts`.

`staff.ts` runs the first employees (#158): `staffMorning()` hires and lets go as `Decisions.staff` wants, with the training and the notice, `payWages()` pays each employee's week on payday, and `crewOf()` says who serves at the truck, delivers and bakes, the owner away all day (`GameState.ownerAway`) or not; `day.ts` calls the three after the bakery's morning, and `service.ts` puts the employees at the truck in line beside the owner. It loads `staff.json` as `STAFF`.

`financing.ts` runs the two expensive ways out of a negative encaisse (#159): `financingOffer()` brings Mme Prudhomme's emergency loan, once a game year, or Max Duvent's offer for shares, the morning after a close below zero, as `tick()` asks before the draw; `takeEmergencyLoan()` and `sellShares()` are what « Accepter » does, each within the rules of `financing.json` however often its effect runs; `financingCharges()` pays each 1st the loans' payments and each quarter the investors' dividends, which `day.ts` posts after the month's charges; and `closingFinancing()` keeps since when the encaisse closes below zero, which `bankruptcy.ts` reads. It loads `financing.json` as `FINANCING` against the event cards it loads itself, and reads no constant of `day.ts` when it loads, so the import cycle through `fixedCosts.ts` stays harmless.

`dream.ts` runs « Le rêve » (#162), the ladder to climb: `applyDecision()` buys a rung that very morning with `{ kind: 'buy', rung }`, a personal one moving the owner's cash (`purchaseMovements()`) and a company one posting its entries (`purchaseEntries()`), the truck that replaces the old one taking it back at its book value (`truckOnBooks()` in `day.ts`). `dreamBonuses()` folds the bonuses of the rungs owned, which `playDay()` applies to the street (`withDreamStreet()`), the counter (`dreamService()`) and a closed day's energy (`dreamEnergy()`), and the decisions to the restock rules (`dreamLimits()`); `housingOf()` replaces the rent, `personalAssetsOn()` counts the owner's rungs in their net worth, `dreamDepreciation()` depreciates the company's on each 1st, `titleOf()` and `nextTitle()` read the titles and `crowned()` sets `magnatOn` at the first close that reaches the last. With nothing bought, a game plays as before, draw for draw.

`places.ts` runs the trips in town (#164): `applyDecision()` sends the owner that very day with `{ kind: 'goTo', place }` (`goTo()`), or keeps them at the truck with a `place` of null, unless `tripRefusal()` says why not. `tripTime()` gives how long a trip takes, the best vehicle of « Le rêve » shortening each walk, and `awayFromCounter()` the window `servers()` gives the owner, while `service.ts` serves nobody with every server away and `customers.ts` lets nobody come then; a place that takes the whole day sets `ownerAway`. A card's `trip` effect books a trip for a later day, which `effects.ts` makes at the start of its day (`wentTo()`) and which the player can neither drop nor trade that morning (`bookedTrip()`, `todaysTrip()`). `playDay()` takes the trip's `energyCost` at the close, the mill's discount off the next restock (`discountAtClose()`), and `tick()` clears the trip and, after one to the Caisse (`loanAppointment()`), has `financingOffer()` bring Mme Prudhomme's appointment. It loads `places.json` as `PLACES`.

`tutorial.ts` runs the tutorial game (#236): `startTutorial()` in `clock.ts` opens it from `tutorial.json`, loaded here as `TUTORIAL`, with its seed, its decisions laid over the starting ones (`tutorialDecisions()`) and its first step (`openingTutorial()`). While it is not done, `isProtected()` holds: `tick()` refuses a day with a step left (`checkTutorialDay()`), brings the card of the tutorial's day (`withScriptedEvent()`) and nothing else, and `playDay()` ends no game. `applyDecision()` marks the step due done with `{ kind: 'tutorialStep', step }` (`completeStep()`); `tutorialStepOf()` gives it, `skipTutorial()` ends the tutorial at once, `endOfProtection()` sets `done` on the morning of `endsDay`, and `tutorialFacts()` reads the last day's croissant shortage and café « trop cher » (`DaySummary.pricedOut`) for the UI.

`bankruptcy.ts` ends a game whose encaisse closed below zero every day of six whole calendar months in a row (#160): `monthsBelowZero()` counts them from `financing.belowZeroSince`, `endAtClose()` gives the `GameState.ended` that `playDay()` sets at the close of the sixth month's last day, after which `playDay()` and `tick()` throw, and `bankruptcyNotice()` brings Mme Prudhomme's letters the mornings after one, three and five months, as `tick()` asks after the financing's cards and before the draw, each once a run, read from the history. None of it draws from the generator or keeps state of its own, and a Défi never goes bankrupt. `netWorth.ts` gives the « Valeur nette » (#161) the top bar, « Aujourd'hui » and the « Faillite » screen show: `netWorthParts()` splits it into the equity less each round of shares sold's share of it, the owner's own cash, their own rungs of « Le rêve » at their price, and their debts, none so far; `netWorth()` is their total; and `netWorthChange()` is how much it moved since the last Sunday's close, which `recordDay()` keeps in `weekHistory`.

`percentiles.ts` tells where an owner stands among the simulated owners of `src/content/percentiles.json`, which `npm run balance -- --percentiles` generates (see `tools/README.md`); the « Bilan de la semaine » shows it through `percentileOnMorning()`. `defiPercentileOf()` tells where a Défi over stands among the simulated games of `src/content/defis-percentiles.json`, which `npm run balance -- --defis` generates; the end of a Défi shows it.
