Every tunable number (prices, costs, pacing, demand) lives here as JSON; no magic numbers in engine code.

`lines.fr.json` holds what a customer says when the player clicks them on the street: each segment's name for one customer, and lines for each way a visit ends (`bought`, `tooExpensive`, `soldOut`, `leftLine`, `browsed`). `lines.ts` validates it: every segment and outcome, at least 8 lines each, none longer than 120 characters or repeated within a segment. Lines name no price, since a line cannot know the real one.

`messages.fr.json` holds the messages the player receives during a game: `landlord` (from « Votre propriétaire ») when the owner's rent goes unpaid, and `exhausted` (from « Vous ») the first time in a game week the owner wakes up exhausted. `messages.ts` validates it: both kinds, at least 5 lines each, none longer than 140 characters or repeated. Lines name no amount, and the owner's are gender-neutral, since the avatar may be a woman or a man.

`avatarFatigue.json` sets how the owner's fatigue shows on the street, which only changes what the world draws: how much slower the arm reaches when tired or exhausted, how long a yawn lasts and how often one comes, how far an exhausted avatar slumps, the colour of the dark circles under their eyes, and where the spill a botched unit leaves on the counter sits, its colour and how long it shows and fades. `avatarFatigue.ts` validates it.

`assets.json` is the world's asset manifest: where each piece of the street and the truck sits, its colour, and the image that replaces its placeholder, and under `light` how the placeholders are lit and what is drawn over the world: the sun and slate colours, the three tones, the cast, contact and ledge shadows, the materials, the grain, the painted unevenness, the vignette and the glow (see `src/world/README.md`). It also sets which pieces show the event cards' changes to the street, and when: a piece's `shows` is `open` or `closed` (days the truck opens or not), `{ "event": "conesOrange" }` (while any change from that card is in effect) or `{ "unavailable": "cafe" }` (while that product cannot be sold); its `text` is what the road sign, the rival truck's board, the festival banner and the « Hors service » card say; and `fans` sets who fans themselves in a heat wave (`shows`, the `chance` of each customer who comes then, and how the fan swings). `assets.ts` validates it: colours as `#RRGGBB`, opacities between 0 and 1, the two blend modes, a shadow layer the manifest lists and no piece is in, a known product in `unavailable`, and a text on the four pieces that write one and on no other. `assets.test.ts` checks that every `event` names a card of `events.json`.

`foodtruck.json` holds the truck's numbers: the products (unit cost, packaging, shelf life), the truck's price and useful life, the monthly costs (permit, parking and commissary; insurance), the fuel per open day, the card payments (the share of sales paid by card and the fee), payday, the walk-ins, the service at the counter, the marketing, and the player's starting decisions and their limits. The `service` sets how long a customer takes at the counter: `baseSeconds` each, plus `perItemSeconds` for each unit ordered, sold out or not; and `maxAheadInLine`, the most customers still waiting a newcomer will stand behind. Each day type's `patienceMultiplier` scales how long its crowd waits. The `marketing` sets what the daily budget brings: awareness is the average spend of the last `awarenessDays` business days, today not counted and a closed day counting as 0, and each open day brings `extraCustomersPerSqrtCent` × √awareness (awareness in cents) walk-ins on top of the day type's crowd, before the day's noise. They come and behave like that crowd, but their segments follow the marketing's `segmentShares`.

`segments.json` holds the customer segments: how long each will wait in line (`patienceMinutes`, before the day type's multiplier), and for each product a reference price, a base purchase probability and a sensitivity.

`difficulty.json` holds the three ways to start a game (Facile, Moyen, Difficile): the cash in the bank once the truck is settled, how the truck comes to the business, and the owner's own savings (`startingPersonalCashCents`). Facile owns the truck; Moyen buys it with a startup loan, whose yearly rate is in basis points (800 is 8 %) and whose term is in months; Difficile leases it by the month. The engine opens the books and charges the truck from these. Its `bakery` holds the bakery's prices at each difficulty: the monthly rent and what the ingredients of one croissant cost, from Meunerie Bonpain. Every difficulty has the middle set of `docs/balance/phase-4.md` until P4-01 adds the contracts and picks one for each.

`bakery.json` holds the bakery, le fournil (#157): the oven, bought (`priceCents`, depreciated over `usefulLifeMonths`) or leased (`monthlyLeaseCents`), the most croissants it makes a day (`capacityPerDay`), the move into its premises (`movingCostCents`), the whole months of rent still due once the player closes it (`noticeMonths`), what a morning of baking takes from the owner's energy (`bakingEnergyCost`), and where the croissants come from, and how many the bakery makes, when a game starts. A croissant made costs only its ingredients: the rent and the oven stay out of its unit cost. `schema.ts` validates it, with the starting bake rule within the capacity. `profile.json` holds the new game screen's defaults (business name, avatar, difficulty) and the longest business name. `schema.ts` validates all three.

`personal.json` holds what the owner's own life costs, paid from their personal account and never by the business: `rentCents` on the 1st of each month, and `weeklyExpensesCents` (groceries, phone, transit) on payday. `schema.ts` validates it.

`energy.json` holds what tires the owner and what fatigue does, on an energy scale of 0 to 100 where a game starts at 100: each open day costs `openDayCost` plus `costPerCustomerServed` for each customer who reaches the counter, each closed day gives `closedDayRecovery` back, unless a card closed it for work (a `closed` effect with `work`), which costs `openDayCost`, and each night gives `nightRecovery`, and each payday the owner's personal cash is below zero takes `debtStress`. Below `tired.below` the owner is « Fatigué », below `exhausted.below` « Épuisé »: serving takes `serviceTimeIncrease` longer (0.2 is 20 %), and `botchedShare` of the units made are botched, written off and made again. `schema.ts` validates it.

`events.json` holds the event cards: dilemmas that come up on a morning and wait for the player's choice before the day is played. Its `pacing` sets how often: no event before game day `firstDay`, at least `minDaysBetween` days from one drawn event to the next (follow-ups do not count), then a chance of `dailyChance` each morning, the event picked by `weight` among those whose conditions hold; a weight of 0 is never drawn. An event with a `date` comes on that day whatever the gap and the chance, if its other conditions hold, unless the follow-up of a `followUpOnly` card is due that morning; another follow-up due then waits a morning. R-06 (#155) set the pacing to day 10, 14 days and 8 %: about one event every 3 to 4 weeks, a median gap of 21 days over two years. `scaling` sets how each difficulty scales what a choice gives and takes (#137): every gain (a cash or a credit sale, the owner's energy or cash going up, a change to the street above 1) by its difficulty's `gains`, every loss (a charge, the owner's energy or cash going down, a change to the street below 1) by its `losses`. Money rounds to the cent and energy to the whole point, half away from zero; a change to the street scales by its distance from 1, so ×0.65 with losses of 1.3 becomes ×0.545, and never below `lowestFactor`; stock, durations, chances and receivable terms never scale. `adaptiveDraw` lists the difficulties whose draw follows the player's standing each morning: « en difficulté » below the `behindBelowPercentile` of their difficulty and week in `percentiles.json`, or with an encaisse below `behindBelowMonthsOfFixedCosts` months of fixed costs; « en avance » above the `aheadAbovePercentile`; « dans la moyenne » otherwise. There, an event's `weightByStanding` (`behind`, `average`, `ahead`) replaces its `weight` for the standings it lists. A Défi ignores both: it plays the cards as written and draws by `weight`. Each event has a camelCase `id`, a `character` (a portrait `id`, a French `name` and `role`), a French `title`, `situation` and optional `quote`, optional `conditions` and two or three `choices`. Every condition given must hold on the morning: `fromDay`, `difficulties`, `months` (1 = January), a `date` (`month` and `dayOfMonth`), `weekdays` (ISO, 1 = Monday), `energyBelow`, `cashAboveCents` and `cashBelowCents` (Encaisse), `oncePerGame`, `maxPerGame` (it came up fewer times than this, follow-ups included), `cooldownDays`, `chosen` and `notChosen` (the player made at least one, or none, of these choices before, each an `event` and one of its `choice`s), `productOnMenu` (its restock rule buys some), `openTomorrow` (`true` when the truck must open the next day, `false` when it must stay closed: the days open hold that weekday and no change to the street, made or queued, closes it) and `followUpOnly` (it only comes as another event's follow-up). A choice has an `id`, a French `label`, the `tags` the card shows before choosing, and its `outcomes`, each with a `chance`, which the player never sees, a French `result` and its `effects`. A tag is a French text shown as it is, or an object: its `text`, shown only at the `difficulties` it lists, if any, and, when it names an `effect`, only where an effect of that `name` applies, with `{montant}` replaced by its amount at the game's difficulty, summed over the effects of that name in the first outcome holding one. A tag never writes an amount by hand, and neither does the rest of the card: the situation, the quote, a choice's label and an outcome's result write `{montant:name}` where the amount of the effects named `name` goes, at the game's difficulty, as a tag would show it. The situation and the quote read the effects of the event's choices, a label those of its choice's outcomes and a result those of its own outcome, in the first outcome holding one; so « La grosse commande » quotes the sale of « Accepter ».

The effects are `charge` (paid now, with a French `description`, to 6900 Autres charges or the expense `account` given: 5200, 6100, 6150, 6200, 6300, 6500 or 6900), `cashSale` (revenue paid now), `creditSale` (revenue now that a named `client` pays `dueInDays` later), `collectReceivable`, `delayReceivable` and `writeOff` (on what a `client` owes), `buyStock` (at the product's unit cost, or at its own `unitCostCents`, then only when the choice is made), `useStock` (oldest units first, to an `order`, a `giveaway` or a `loss`, to 5100 Pertes sur stocks), `energy` (held between 0 and 100), `personal` (the owner's own cash, with a French `label`, outside the books), `followUp` (another event `days` later, with a `chance`, 1 by default), `headline` (a La Presse-Citron headline's id), and the changes to the street, each for `days` days from the day it starts: `walkIns` (multiplies the walk-ins of everyone, or of one `segment`, by a `factor`: 0.45 for the first week of construction, 1.8 for a festival), `appeal` (multiplies one `product`'s appeal: 0.3 for chocolat chaud in a heat wave), `willingness` (multiplies what everyone, or one `segment`, will pay: 1.1 on moving day), `unavailable` (a `product` cannot be sold) and `closed` (the truck stays closed, whatever the days open; with `work: true`, the owner works that day away from the counter, so it tires like an open day with no customer instead of resting them). Every `factor` is above 0, and the changes in effect on a day multiply each other. An effect runs when the choice is made, or later: after `inDays` days, or on the next ISO weekday `onNextWeekday` (4 is next Thursday, a week away on a Thursday), never both. It applies at every difficulty, or only at the `difficulties` it lists, and may carry a `name` for its choice's tags. `schema.ts` validates it: besides each field, the event ids are unique, the choice ids unique within their event, each choice's chances sum to 1, every follow-up names an event of the file, every choice `chosen` and `notChosen` list is one of the file's, a tag showing `{montant}` names its effect, every effect a tag names is one of its choice's, of one unit (money, energy or a percentage) when the tag shows its amount, and every `{montant:name}` of a card's text names effects of one unit where it looks, at every difficulty, with no other braces in the text. So that the card fits, a situation holds at most 280 characters, a choice's label 45 and a result 200. The first card, `essai`, tries every effect for the tests; its weight of 0 keeps it out of a real game.

The twelve cards of P3-05 (#93) follow it, each teaching one business idea without saying so: `grosseCommande` (a big order of 400 croissants paid in 60 days, or a little less paid on delivery, either one closing the truck on Thursday to deliver it), `chequeDansLaMalle` (its follow-up, on the morning the money is due, when the client is late), `conesOrange` (construction outside the truck), `machineTousse` (repair now, later or with tape), `critique` and `influenceuse` (croissants given away for publicity: each giveaway buys the croissants before giving them, since none are left from the day before), `concurrence` (a poutine truck across the street), `formulaire14B` (pay a fine or contest it), `chalet` (a long weekend off, Friday to Sunday, on a summer morning the owner is not fully rested: #155 dropped its weekdays and gave it a weight of 4, so it comes in most games), `canicule` (a heat wave), `festival` (a three-day festival) and `premierJuillet` (moving day, on a June 30 whose July 1 the truck opens). Each has a weight of 1. P3-13 (#101) tuned their amounts, chances and effects at Moyen with `npm run balance -- --percentiles`, until no choice is best on both the encaisse and the bénéfice net, 8 weeks on, in more than 80 % of the games its card comes up in, nor best on neither in more than 85 %: see `docs/balance/phase-3.md`, where the table of every choice is. A choice's tags say what the owner would know before choosing; one that leaves something to chance, with several outcomes or a follow-up whose chance is under 1, says « Risqué » and never the odds. At Difficile, every outcome of every choice costs something, refusing included: P3-13b (#137) gave the six that cost nothing an effect of Difficile only, and the tags « Rien ne change » and « Gratuit » show only at Facile and Moyen. The four cards of #137 follow, drawn at every difficulty with a weight of 1, and at Facile by the player's standing: `gateauFete` (a birthday order paid in cash on Saturday, which opens the truck late that day) and `traiteurPresse` (a caterer whose oven died, paying cash while the line waits), weighted toward « en difficulté », and `placementMiracle` (a « guaranteed » investment of the owner's own money) and `peintureLuxe` (a metallic paint job that brings the chic customers), weighted toward « en avance ». Their characters have no portrait yet. #137 then tuned every card and the `scaling` at each difficulty with the same report, until each difficulty meets the targets of #101; `placementMiracle`, which moves only the owner's own money, is judged on it. Each character's `id` names their portrait in `src/ui/portraits/`. R-06 (#155) capped `influenceuse` and `concurrence` at two a game and `machineTousse` at three, and added thirteen cards, each queuing a La Presse-Citron headline, whose amounts are starting values a balance run will tune: `panneCourant` (a power cut: rent a generator or lose the café and chocolate stock), `stagiaire` (Normand's nephew: some energy back, slower service, and maybe the hot chocolate reserve gone), `feteQuartier` (sponsor the street party's banner), `fournisseurRetard` (no croissant delivery tomorrow: close, pay the dépanneur's price or open without), `carteFidelite` (loyalty cards), `inspection` (clean all day or risk a fine), `pluie` and `pluieAuvent` (three days of rain; once the awning is bought, the rain comes as the second card, which halves the loss), `clientMystere` (a critic incognito), `camionVoisin` (the neighbour's café truck closes: buy its café cheap or its espresso machine, after which `machineTousse` never comes again), `commandite` (the pee-wee hockey team), `volCaisse` (a theft: buy a safe or risk another, the card coming back 30 days later at 10 %) and `reportage` (a day closed for an interview). The awning, the safe and the machine are charged as expenses: an event cannot buy equipment. `eventCards.test.ts` plays every outcome of every choice of the cards in a seed-42 game, from the first morning the card may come up, and `eventPacing.test.ts` plays 100 games of two years at Moyen with random choices to check the pacing #155 asks for.

`defis.json` holds the Défis: short games with one goal and a medal, each 1 to 9 weeks. Each has a camelCase `id`, a French `title` and `story` (at most 400 characters), a `difficulty`, a `start` date in the first season of year 1 (`month` and `dayOfMonth`, from May 1st) and a `lengthDays`. Its `setup` replaces how a game of its difficulty opens: `cashCents`, the cash in the bank once the truck is settled, in place of the difficulty's `startingCashCents`; `prices` and `restock`, product by product, each product keeping its kind of restock rule; `daysOpen`, `ownerWeeklyPayCents` and `marketingDailyCents`; and the owner's `energy` on the first morning. `locked` lists the kinds of decision the player cannot change: `price`, `restock`, `daysOpen`, `ownerWeeklyPay` or `marketingDaily`. `modifiers` force changes to the street, each `change` a street change as an event's effect writes it without its `days`, for `days` days from the Défi's day `day`, counting from 0, made as if by a choice of the card `event`, so the world shows it. `events` force a card of `events.json` on the morning of a day, one a day at most, where the player may make only the `choices` listed. `randomEvents` lets the event cards' draws come too; follow-ups always do. The `goal` measures one `metric` over the Défi, `lowestCash` (the lowest Encaisse after any entry, in cents), `netIncome` (the bénéfice net), `sales` (the ventes), `customersServed`, `spoilageRate` (the units written off over those sold and written off) or `lowestEnergy`, and its `medals` give the least each of `or`, `argent` and `bronze` needs, or the most for `spoilageRate`, where lower is better. Its `conditions` must hold all along, or the Défi ends at once, « échoué »: `minCash` (`amountCents`), `minEnergy` (`amount`) and `maxSpoilageRate` (`rate`), each once at most. `schema.ts` validates it against `events.json`: besides each field, the ids are unique, the medals in order, the forced days within the Défi, and every forced card and choice one of `events.json`'s. The six of P3-11 (#99) are starting values that P3-14 (#102) tunes; `defis.test.ts` plays one scripted success and one scripted failure of each.

`percentiles.json` holds how 1 000 simulated owners did at each difficulty, for « Mieux que N % des propriétaires »: for each week from 1 to 104, their bénéfice net from day 0 to the close of the week's Sunday at the percentiles 5, 10, 20 … 90 and 95, in cents. It is generated, not written by hand: `npm run balance -- --percentiles` writes it with `docs/balance/phase-3.md` (see `tools/README.md`), and it goes stale whenever the engine or the content moves the numbers. `schema.ts` validates it: whole percentiles strictly between 0 and 100, going up, and each week one amount per percentile, never going down. `src/engine/percentiles.ts` reads it.

`headlines.fr.json` holds the La Presse-Citron headlines the ticker shows (`src/ui/headlines.ts` reads it): `events`, the text of each headline a card's `headline` effect queues, by its id, one for each id the cards use and no other, and `general`, the 20 everyday headlines the ticker cycles through, each one line of at most 120 characters. They mock parody institutions, name real places and are never mean about a group of people.

`report.json` sets what the « Bilan de la semaine » tells of a week (`src/engine/week.ts` reads it): at most `maxFacts` facts, the highest `weight` first, except that a bénéfice up while the encaisse is not always comes first. Each fact has its weight and when it holds: `profitUpCashDown`, a bénéfice net above 0 while the encaisse went down, or went up at least `minGapCents` less than the bénéfice, as when a big order is paid in 60 days; `unpaidRent`, the owner's rent went unpaid; `receivableDue`, a customer owes something due within `withinDays` of the Sunday; `spoilage`, the stock written off cost more than `aboveCents`; `bestWeek`, ventes above every earlier week of the game; `lostToLine`, more than `aboveVisits` visits left the line; and `daysOpenInARow`, the truck opened at least `atLeast` days in a row. A tie between equal weights goes in that order. `schema.ts` validates it: every fact, and whole numbers of at least 0, but at least 1 for `maxFacts` and `atLeast`.

`report.fr.json` holds what the « Bilan de la semaine » says (`src/ui/weeklyReport.ts` reads it): `facts`, the text of each fact of `report.json`, at least 2 variants each, the weeks taking them in turn, each naming every number of its fact as `{name}` (`profitUpCashDown`: `{netIncome}` and `{cashChange}`, written with its sign; `unpaidRent`: `{day}`; `receivableDue`: `{client}`, `{amount}` and `{dueDay}`; `spoilage`: `{cost}` and `{units}`; `bestWeek`: `{sales}` and `{previousBest}`; `lostToLine`: `{visits}`; `daysOpenInARow`: `{days}`); `headlines`, La Presse-Citron's on a week whose first fact is that one, at least 2 each, which may name the fact's numbers; `quietHeadlines`, at least 2, for a week with no fact; `advice`, « Le conseil de Mme Prudhomme », the banker's dry one-liner on a week whose first fact is that one, at least 3 each, which may name the fact's numbers and points at the thing to look at without saying why; `quietAdvice`, at least 5 and with no number, hers for a week with no fact; and `creditSaleNote`, the chart's note on the day of a sale on credit, with `{amount}`, `{client}` and `{dueDay}`. `reportText.ts` validates it: every fact, no other number and no stray brace, facts and advice of at most 160 characters and headlines of at most 120, and no line twice. The counts are always 2 or more, as `reportText.test.ts` checks against the thresholds of `report.json`, so they are written in the plural. The text describes the week and never explains a term.

`pacing.json` sets how fast the game runs: `msPerDayAtNormalSpeed` at normal speed, the `speeds` the top bar offers (0 is the pause) and `maxTicksPerFrame`, the most days one frame plays. It also sets how the « Bilan de la semaine » shows: in full, pausing the game, for the first `fullReportWeeks` weeks of a game, then as a card that slides in for `reportSlideInMs` of real time.

`cashGauge.json` sets when the top bar's cash reads low: below this many months of what every truck pays monthly.
